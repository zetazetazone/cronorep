CronoRep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

CronoRep is a rest timer for gym workouts on Wear OS watches and Android phones. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data we collect

We do not sell data. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

The Android apps send two things to our server: a scrambled device ID and the free trial time you have used. The scrambled ID is made from the Android device ID with a one-way function (SHA-256), so we cannot see the real device ID. We use this data only so that deleting and installing the app again does not restart the free trial. The server also records whether the device is a watch or a phone, and when the data was first and last saved. It contains no name, email or account. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The server sees your IP address. We use it only to limit abuse, and we do not store it with this data. We send nothing after you unlock the app. We keep the data while the app is offered. To ask us to delete it, email us at the address on the store listing and include the "Trial code" shown in the app settings.

The Apple Watch app sends no data to us.

Data saved on your devices

The app saves your rest time, your settings, the free trial time you have used and your unlock status on your devices. On Apple Watch, the free trial time is kept in the Keychain, so it can stay after you delete the app.

Phone and watch

If you use the phone app and the watch app, the phone sends only your look settings (minimalist mode, save battery mode and background choice) to your own watch. They go through Google Play services on your devices. They do not go to us.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

GIF backgrounds (optional)

If you turn on GIF backgrounds, or open the GIF chooser in the phone app, the app downloads animated images from GIPHY. GIPHY then receives your IP address and basic device information, as with any internet request. The app sends no other data to GIPHY. GIF backgrounds are off by default. See the GIPHY Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Vibration: to tell you when your rest time ends.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep the timer exact when the screen is off.
  • Internet: for Google Play purchases and the optional GIF backgrounds.
  • Notifications: to show that a timer is running.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page.
