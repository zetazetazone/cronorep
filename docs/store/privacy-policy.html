CronoRep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

CronoRep is a rest timer for gym workouts on Wear OS watches. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Data that stays on your watch

The app saves your rest time, your settings, the free trial time you have used and your unlock status. This data stays on your watch. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment. We do not receive your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

GIF backgrounds (optional)

If you turn on GIF backgrounds, the app downloads animated images from GIPHY. GIPHY then receives your IP address and basic device information, as with any internet request. The app sends no other data to GIPHY. GIF backgrounds are off by default. See the GIPHY Privacy Policy.

Permissions

  • Vibration: to tell you when your rest time ends.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep the timer exact when the screen is off.
  • Internet: for Google Play purchases and the optional GIF backgrounds.
  • Notifications: to show that a timer is running.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page.
